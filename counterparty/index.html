    How it works
    • The contract settles at a hidden whole number from 20 to 80. The street estimate is usually within about 8 of it.
    • Each round you quote a bid and an ask for 1 lot. One counterparty arrives and either hits your bid, lifts your ask, or passes.
    • Noise traders buy or sell at random. They trade only if your spread is tight enough for them, so wide quotes scare them off.
    • Tipped traders know the value roughly. Informed traders know it exactly and trade only when your quote is wrong.
    • You never see who you traded with until the end. Watch which side keeps getting hit and move your quotes.
    • Your position limit is ±8. At the limit, the side that would add to your position is pulled.
    • Final P&L = cash + position × settlement value. The same seed and difficulty always give the same value and the same counterparties.
